Hotels

Where you’ll be staying.

The Bank Hotel

Istanbul · 3–6 November

Arap Cami, Bankalar Cd. No:5 D:1, 34421 Beyoğlu/İstanbul, Türkiye

In Karaköy, with views of the historic peninsula and a rooftop restaurant overlooking old Istanbul. It sits in an artists’ quarter and is well placed for bohemian Beyoğlu and Sultanahmet.

thebankhotelistanbul.com

Ergon House Athens

Athens · 6–10 November

23 Mitropoleos Street, Athina 105 57, Greece

An epicurean boutique hotel at the foot of the Acropolis, with a modern-day agora of greengrocers, a butcher, fishmonger and roastery, plus a bar, restaurant, gym and a roof terrace with cocktails.

houses.ergonfoods.com

Istanbul Airport to The Bank Hotel

About 40 minutes in normal traffic.

Yellow airport taxi

Grab one from the rank outside the IST terminal. Roughly 230 ₺ – 350 ₺ on standard routes.

Private transfer

Pre-book door-to-door through The Bank Hotel Istanbul or a local operator. Roughly €35 – €50 (about $50 USD).

Important notes

Read before you pack.

Travel insurance is strongly recommended.

Equipment. Bring whatever camera you want. Your host travels light and usually shoots one camera. You’ll be on and off trains and in and out of taxis, so use a small bag if you’re bringing multiple lenses.

WhatsApp. A group will carry phone and location details through the workshop. Download the app if you don’t already use it.

eSIMs. Choose a multi-country regional eSIM, or two single-country eSIMs. Turkey blocks many international eSIM activation pages after arrival, so set it up before you land.

Local customs. A good read on the subtle customs of Istanbul: studyinturkiye.com
